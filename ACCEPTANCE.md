# Acceptance criteria — SC01–SC54

Statuses: **Pass** (required behaviour verified at the appropriate level), **Fail**, **Partial**, **Blocked**
(needs credentials, infrastructure or a policy decision), **Not verified** (implemented but not yet verifiable
here). A criterion that depends on a live Google or AI service cannot be **Pass** from mocked tests alone.

Baseline taken on 15 October 2026 at commit `4938680` (see REVIEW.md §2). Final status recorded in Phase 7 the same day.

**Final-status rule:** *Pass* = the behaviour lies entirely in this repository's logic and is verified by automated tests (pure functions, in-memory Sheets fakes, jsdom or headless Chromium). Anything whose required behaviour depends on live Classroom, Drive content, Docs, AI services, triggers, real devices or a deployment is *Not verified* (implemented, awaiting live verification) or *Blocked* (needs a decision or infrastructure). Live procedures: [docs/LIVE_TESTING.md](docs/LIVE_TESTING.md).
